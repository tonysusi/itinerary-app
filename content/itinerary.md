## Sat 20 Jun 2026

- **Location:** Mount Rainier Paradise → Tacoma → Whidbey Island
- **Activity:**
  - Drive to Paradise — ~30 min from Ashford — arrive early for parking (no timed entry required in 2026)

- **Note:** ❄️ Late June at Paradise (5,400 ft): expect snow on upper Skyline Trail. Lower trails like Nisqually Vista and Myrtle Falls will be clear. Bring layers and waterproof boots — weather changes fast at altitude. National Park entrance fee: $30/vehicle.
